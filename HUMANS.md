# Humans and Machines

This is a library that helps machines talk to other machines (ATProto OAuth).
The humans who maintain it are trying to make the machines' jobs easier.

When filing issues or sending PRs, consider that the humans reviewing them are spending time they could be writing code. Be specific, be kind, and include all relevant context.

## Code of Conduct

- Assume competence
- Ask questions early
- Be patient with documentation gaps
- Submit small, focused changes over large, sweeping refactors
